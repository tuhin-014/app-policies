QuranKids Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 20, 2026

Developer: Vertex Lab Solutions (Md Hasan)

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

QuranKids is designed for children ages 4-14. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data.

  • No names, emails, or contact information are transmitted
  • No location data is collected
  • No analytics or tracking is used
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • All data stays on your device only

Speech Recognition

QuranKids uses Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to check pronunciation. Audio is processed locally on your device and is NOT sent to any server.

Local Storage

The app stores learning progress (letters learned, stars earned, achievements) locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

QuranKids offers optional subscriptions managed through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

No Advertising

QuranKids contains NO advertising of any kind.

No Social Features

There are no social features, messaging, or user-generated content in this app.

Parent Controls

The app includes a PIN-protected parent dashboard where parents can view their child's progress.
