🛡️ COPPA Compliance

QuranKids is designed for children ages 4-14. We are fully committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations.

We do NOT collect any personal information from children.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ No names, emails, or personal data sent to servers
  • ❌ No location tracking
  • ❌ No photos or media access
  • ❌ No advertising or ad tracking
  • ❌ No social features or user accounts
  • ❌ No analytics or third-party tracking
  • ❌ No cookies or web tracking

Data Stored Locally on Device

QuranKids stores the following data only on your device:

  • Child's first name (for personalized greetings)
  • Age group selection
  • Lesson progress and scores
  • Achievement badges
  • Daily streak data

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

QuranKids uses the device microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition framework to check pronunciation during the "Listen & Repeat" feature. Audio is:

  • Processed on-device when possible
  • Never stored or uploaded to our servers
  • Used solely for real-time pronunciation feedback

In-App Purchases

QuranKids offers optional subscription purchases processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Information

The Parent Dashboard is protected by a PIN code. Parents can view their child's learning progress, pronunciation accuracy, and study habits. All this data is stored locally on the device.

Third-Party Services

QuranKids does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all QuranKids data by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
