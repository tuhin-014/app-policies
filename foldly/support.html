Foldly · folding help and answers

Getting started

Foldly is not a tapping game. You need a square of paper in your hand. Watch a fold happen on screen, pause whenever you like, then make the same fold in real paper. The app waits for you.

No square paper? Every project has a Only have printer paper? link that shows how to square a sheet in two folds.

Common questions

My fold does not look like the screen.

Tap the turtle to slow the animation down, or Replay to watch the step again. Crease firmly and line corners up before you press — most folds that drift are a corner that was a few millimetres out two steps ago.

How do I turn the voice off?

The speaker button under the animation turns narration on and off, and it remembers your choice.

I bought the unlock and my projects are locked again.

Open any locked project, pass the grown-ups step, and tap Restore a purchase. This happens on a new device or after reinstalling — the purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not the phone.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, once, and it never renews. Three projects are free whether you buy or not.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. Anything that spends money is behind a step that asks for four digits written as words, which a child who cannot yet read cannot pass.

Does Foldly need the internet?

No. Every project and every spoken instruction is already on the device. The only thing that ever needs a connection is making or restoring the purchase, which Apple handles.

Still stuck?

Write to support@vertexlabsolutions.com and say which project and which step. If a fold is wrong, we want to know — tell us and we will check it against real paper.
