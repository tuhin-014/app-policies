Both files stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and this page has no server to upload them to.

Where these files come from

In CleanVerify, open More → Export proof archive. The archive contains Photos/ and Timestamps/. A timestamp file is named after the room and whether it is the before or after photo.

What this page can and cannot tell you

A timestamp token is a receipt from an independent timestamp authority saying a particular fingerprint was shown to it at a particular moment. This page reads that time out of the token, recomputes the photo's fingerprint, and tells you whether they match.

It does not verify the authority's signature. That needs the authority's certificate chain and is beyond what a page like this should claim. To check the signature itself, use OpenSSL on your own machine: 

openssl ts -reply -in timestamp.tsr -text
shasum -a 256 photo.jpg

A timestamp shows one thing: that this exact file existed by that moment. It does not show who took the photo, where it was taken, or that any work was carried out. The photo itself was never uploaded to the authority — only its fingerprint.
