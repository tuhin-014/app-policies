Neurolark — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Neurolark ("the app") is made by VertexLab Solutions. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your game results stay on your iPhone, and we do not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

Game results, levels, streaks, your goals, your optional first name and your reminder time are stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults. We do not run servers for Neurolark and do not receive this information. Deleting the app, or using Profile > Delete all progress, removes it.

What we collect

Nothing. Neurolark does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and does not create an account.

Microphone and speech recognition

The speaking games (Fluency Sprint, Clear Speech and Say It) use the microphone and Apple's Speech framework to turn what you say into text so the app can count items, check pronunciation and measure pace, pauses and filler words. Audio is not recorded or saved by Neurolark. On iPhones that support on-device recognition, speech is processed entirely on the device. On devices that do not, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy; Neurolark never receives it. You can decline microphone access and type answers instead, or turn speaking games off in Profile.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder or Word of the Day, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Neurolark Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Manage or cancel any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Health information

Neurolark is for practice and enjoyment. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. Skill scores describe performance in the app's games only.

Children

Neurolark is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app.
