Neurolark: Brain & Speech Gym · help and answers

Getting started

Open the Today tab and tap Start workout. The daily workout is a few short games picked for you, and it is always free. You can choose 3, 4 or 5 games a day.

Prefer no clock? Use the Untimed workout on Today, or the Unwind filter on the Games tab, where timed games open untimed.

Common questions

What is free, and what does Premium add?

The daily workout is free, and 8 games can be played any time for free. Premium opens every game whenever you like. Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Neurolark. Apple handles all billing, so we cannot cancel or refund for you. Refunds can be requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid, but games are locked again.

Go to Profile and tap Restore purchases. This is needed on a new phone or after reinstalling. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account, not the phone.

The speaking games can't hear me.

Check that Neurolark has microphone and speech recognition access in Settings > Privacy & Security. Speak in a quiet place, close to the phone. Fluency Sprint also lets you type answers instead, and you can turn speaking games off in Profile.

Are my recordings saved?

No. Neurolark turns speech into text while you play and does not keep the audio. See the privacy policy.

Can I start over?

Profile > Delete all progress removes your results, levels and streak from this iPhone. It can't be undone.

Is Neurolark a medical or therapy app?

No. Neurolark is for practice and enjoyment. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, including dementia, ADHD or cognitive decline. Speaking games are not speech therapy. If you are worried about your memory or speech, talk to a doctor.

Still stuck?

Write to support@vertexlabsolutions.com and tell us which game and what happened.
