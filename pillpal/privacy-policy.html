PillPal Pro: Never Miss a Dose — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

Overview

PillPal Pro ("the App") is developed by VertexLab Solutions. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data Collection

PillPal Pro stores all your data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your personal health information on any external servers.

Data stored locally includes:

  • Medication names, doses, and schedules
  • Dose logs (taken, skipped, missed)
  • Family group information
  • Doctor appointment details
  • Medical ID information (allergies, conditions, blood type)
  • User profile and preferences

HealthKit Integration

If you choose to enable HealthKit sync, PillPal Pro may write medication intake data to Apple Health. This data is governed by Apple's HealthKit privacy policies. HealthKit integration is entirely optional and controlled by you in Settings.

Family Sharing

If you use the Family Monitoring feature, medication adherence data may be shared between family group members via Apple's CloudKit. This data remains within Apple's ecosystem and is protected by Apple's privacy standards.

Notifications

PillPal Pro uses local notifications to remind you about medications and appointments. These notifications are processed entirely on your device.

Third-Party Services

PillPal Pro does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with any third parties.

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the app from your device. Since data is stored locally, deleting the app permanently removes all your information.

Children's Privacy

PillPal Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
