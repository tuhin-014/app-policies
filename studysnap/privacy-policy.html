Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 20, 2026

StudySnap ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

Camera & Photo Library Access: StudySnap accesses your device camera and photo library solely to capture or import images of homework problems for solving. Images are processed on-device or sent to our AI service for analysis. We do not store your images on our servers after processing is complete.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage analytics (e.g., feature usage frequency, session duration) to improve the app experience. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

In-App Purchases: Subscription and purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily contact us.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide homework solving, flashcard generation, and study features.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.
  • To process in-app subscription access.

4. Data Storage & Security

Study history, flashcards, and progress data are stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in storage frameworks. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect any data transmitted to our services.

5. Children's Privacy

StudySnap is designed to be safe for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app includes a Parent Dashboard feature with PIN protection for parental oversight.

6. Third-Party Services

StudySnap may use third-party AI services (such as OpenAI) to process homework images and generate solutions. Images sent to these services are used solely for generating responses and are not retained by third-party providers beyond their standard processing periods.

7. Your Rights

  • You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • You can manage camera and photo library permissions in your device Settings.
  • You can request information about your data by contacting us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
