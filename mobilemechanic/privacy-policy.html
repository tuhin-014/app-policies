Mobile Mechanic Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 16, 2026

Introduction

Mobile Mechanic Pro ("the App") is a job management application for mobile mechanics on iOS. Your privacy is fundamental to how Mobile Mechanic Pro is designed. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Mobile Mechanic Pro collects zero user data. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to any server. All business data you enter (clients, vehicles, jobs, invoices) is stored exclusively on your device.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. No data ever leaves your device. There is no cloud sync, no remote database, and no server-side component.

Third-Party Services

Mobile Mechanic Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. There are no SDKs that collect or transmit user data.

Account & Authentication

Mobile Mechanic Pro does not require an account. There is no sign-up, login, or authentication of any kind.

Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera — To take before/after photos of work performed on vehicles.
  • Photo Library — To attach existing photos to job records.

These permissions operate entirely on-device. Photos are stored locally and are never uploaded or transmitted.

In-App Purchases

Mobile Mechanic Pro offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions and a lifetime purchase to unlock Pro features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple ID settings.

Data Sharing

Mobile Mechanic Pro does not share any data with any third party. There is no data to share because no data is collected.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, clearing the App's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

Mobile Mechanic Pro does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
