Getting started

How does my child use it?

Tap a block in the drawer and it joins the program. Blocks snap under each other like puzzle pieces and run from the top down. Press Play and the character does exactly what the blocks say. There's a full walkthrough inside the app — tap How to play on the Home screen, or the ? button in the editor.

What's the purple slider?

That's Rewind, and it's the part we're proudest of. Drag it backwards and the character walks back across the stage, the highlighted block moves back through the program, and the score counts back down. When something doesn't work, rewinding shows a child exactly where what happened stopped matching what they meant.

My child is stuck.

Tap Hint. The first hint is a nudge, the second points at the right drawer, and the third names the block that's missing — it never just gives the answer. The bug button explains what the program is actually doing, which is usually the faster route.

For grown-ups

Where are the parent controls?

You → Grown-ups. You'll be asked to read four numbers written as words and type them as digits — that's the parental gate. Inside you'll find what your child has actually learned (measured from the code in their projects, not from minutes on task), mission progress, controls for sharing, hints and sound, and data deletion.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tinkerbolt works entirely offline. There is no networking code in the app at all.

Does my child need an account?

No — and they can't make one. There's no sign-in, no password and no email, which also means there's nothing they can be locked out of.

How do I delete everything?

You → Grown-ups → Delete this profile and all its projects. It's immediate and local. Deleting the app also removes everything.

How do I see the welcome again?

You → Grown-ups → Play the intro again. Projects and progress are kept.

Is there a subscription?

No. Tinkerbolt is free, with no in-app purchases and no adverts.

Accessibility

Tinkerbolt supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion. Every block is identified by colour and an icon and a distinct notch shape, so blocks stay tellable apart without relying on colour vision.
