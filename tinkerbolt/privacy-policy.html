Privacy Policy — Tinkerbolt

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · Provided by VertexLab Solutions LLC ("we", "us")
Tinkerbolt is made for children. It has no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics of any kind, and it contains no networking code at all — the app cannot send anything anywhere, because there is nothing in it that can make a network request.

The short version

We collect nothing. Not a name, not an email address, not a birthday, not a device identifier. Your child does not create an account because Tinkerbolt does not have accounts. Everything they make stays on the iPad or iPhone it was made on.

The age band is chosen by a grown-up and is stored as one of three values. We do not ask for a date of birth, and we do not want one.

What the app cannot do

  • No camera and no microphone. Tinkerbolt does not request these permissions, so iOS will never prompt you for them and the app has no way to record anything.
  • No location. Never requested.
  • No contacts, photos or files. Never requested.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • No third-party code. No analytics SDK, no ad network, no crash reporter, no social login. The app is built only from Apple's own frameworks and our own code.

Children's privacy (COPPA and the Kids Category)

Tinkerbolt is designed for the Apple App Store Kids Category. Apple's guidelines require that apps in that category do not send personally identifiable information or device information to third parties, and do not include third-party analytics or advertising. Tinkerbolt meets this by having no third-party code and no network capability whatsoever.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for which we would need verifiable parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, and nothing to disclose, sell, share or retain.

Purchases, external links and the grown-ups area sit behind a parental gate. Note that a parental gate is a control on what a child can reach inside the app; it is not the same thing as consent to collect data — which we avoid needing by not collecting any.

Deleting everything

Open You → Grown-ups, pass the parental gate, and choose Delete this profile and all its projects. That removes the profile, its progress and every project it made, immediately and on the device. Deleting the app removes everything as well. Because none of it was ever sent to us, there is no copy anywhere else and no request you need to send us.

Purchases

Tinkerbolt is free and contains no in-app purchases at this time. If that ever changes, purchases would be handled by Apple, would sit behind a parental gate, and this page would be updated before the change shipped.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes.
