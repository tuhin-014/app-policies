Privacy Policy — Tinkerblox

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · Provided by VertexLab Solutions LLC ("we", "us")
Tinkerblox is made for children. It has no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics of any kind, and it contains no networking code at all — the app cannot send anything anywhere, because there is nothing in it that can make a network request.

The short version

We collect nothing. Not a name, not an email address, not a birthday, not a device identifier. Your child does not create an account because Tinkerblox does not have accounts. Everything they make stays on the iPad or iPhone it was made on, unless a grown-up chooses to share a creation — and even then it goes straight to the person you pick, never to us. We run no servers, so there is nowhere for it to be uploaded to.

Sharing a creation

A child can send something they built to a friend or a relative. This is the only way anything leaves the device, and it works like sending a photo: the creation becomes a file, and Apple's own share sheet hands it to whoever you choose — AirDrop, Messages, Files. There is no Tinkerblox server, no gallery to publish to, no profile, no follower list and no way for anyone to contact your child through the app.

  • A grown-up has to approve it. Sharing sits behind a parental gate every time, and a grown-up can switch it off completely in You → Grown-ups.
  • The file contains the creation and nothing else — the blocks, the characters, a name and the child's generated display name (e.g. PixelDragon42). No account, no email, no device identifier, no age band, no location.
  • Names are chosen from our word lists, not typed. A child picks two words, so a creation's name cannot contain anything we did not write.
  • Words in speech bubbles are filtered against a fixed vocabulary. Anything outside it is replaced with “...” before the file is created, and the child is told so. This runs in both directions, so a creation arriving from elsewhere is filtered on the way in as well. The practical effect is that arbitrary text written by a stranger cannot reach a child through this app.
  • Receiving is safe by the same design. An arriving file is checked and repaired before it opens, and anything unrecognisable in it is discarded rather than run.

Once a file has left the device it is out of our hands and out of yours, exactly as a photo sent in Messages would be. We never see it either way.

What the app cannot do

  • No camera and no microphone. Tinkerblox does not request these permissions, so iOS will never prompt you for them and the app has no way to record anything.
  • No location. Never requested.
  • No contacts, photos or files. Never requested.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • No third-party code. No analytics SDK, no ad network, no crash reporter, no social login. The app is built only from Apple's own frameworks and our own code.

Children's privacy (COPPA and the Kids Category)

Tinkerblox is designed for the Apple App Store Kids Category. Apple's guidelines require that apps in that category do not send personally identifiable information or device information to third parties, and do not include third-party analytics or advertising. Tinkerblox meets this by having no third-party code and by making no network connections of its own: the app never contacts a server, ours or anyone else's. Sharing a creation is handled entirely by Apple's share sheet, which delivers the file to the recipient a grown-up picks; nothing passes through us.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for which we would need verifiable parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, and nothing to disclose, sell, share or retain.

Purchases, external links and the grown-ups area sit behind a parental gate. Note that a parental gate is a control on what a child can reach inside the app; it is not the same thing as consent to collect data — which we avoid needing by not collecting any.

Deleting everything

Open You → Grown-ups, pass the parental gate, and choose Delete this profile and all its projects. That removes the profile, its progress and every project it made, immediately and on the device. Deleting the app removes everything as well. Because none of it was ever sent to us, there is no copy anywhere else and no request you need to send us. If a creation was shared with someone, that copy belongs to them and is not ours to delete — ask them, the same as you would about a photo.

Purchases

Tinkerblox is free and contains no in-app purchases at this time. If that ever changes, purchases would be handled by Apple, would sit behind a parental gate, and this page would be updated before the change shipped.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes.
