HomeVault - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Overview

HomeVault is a home inventory documentation tool developed by Vertex Lab Solutions. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

HomeVault does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data is stored locally on your device only.

Information Stored Locally

  • Home profile (name, type, address)
  • Room information and photos
  • Item details (name, category, value, photos)
  • Barcode scan data
  • Receipt and warranty photos

AI Room Scanner

When you use the AI Room Scanner feature, your room photo is sent to Google Gemini API for analysis. The photo is processed to detect items and is not stored by the AI service beyond the request.

Camera & Photo Library

HomeVault uses your camera and photo library to photograph belongings. Photos are stored locally within the app.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party services used are: Apple StoreKit for subscriptions (governed by Apple's privacy policy) and Google Gemini API for AI room scanning (photos sent for analysis only, not stored).

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

Children's Privacy

HomeVault is not directed at children under 13.
