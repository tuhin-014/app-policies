Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Overview

BizShield ("the App") is built with privacy as a core principle. All data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers or third parties.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data — including business profiles, generated documents, compliance deadlines, tax estimates, and contractor information — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Document Generation

All legal documents are generated locally on your device using built-in templates. No user data is sent to any third-party service, AI service, or external API for document generation. The App does not use OpenAI, GPT, or any other AI service.

Third-Party Services

The App uses only Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscriptions. Apple's privacy practices apply to purchase transactions. No other third-party services, APIs, or SDKs are used.

Subscriptions

BizShield offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly plans) to unlock premium features. Subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your device's Settings > Subscriptions.

Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not use cookies, device fingerprinting, or any form of user tracking.

Data Export & Deletion

You can delete all data from within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Legal Disclaimer

BizShield is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Documents and compliance information are templates and general guidance only. Consult a qualified attorney for your specific legal needs.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.
